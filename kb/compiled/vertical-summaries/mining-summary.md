## Our Positioning

- **Target ICP**: Mid-market miners, R$100M--R$2B revenue
- **Core problem**: 5--8 disconnected systems (ERP, mine planning, FMS, LIMS, environmental) with no data flow between them
- **Entry strategy**: Compliance-first -- CFEM miscalculations and environmental license risk create urgency that bypasses IT budget cycles
- **Differentiator**: Integration expertise across siloed systems that no single vendor addresses holistically

## Key Offerings

## Competitive Advantage

1. **Mandatory digital compliance**: ANM's PGRM platform (March 2025) forces digital CFEM/DIEF submission -- creates immediate demand
2. **Mid-market focus**: Too large for spreadsheets, too small for Vale-scale teams -- aligned with BNDES R$1B fund
3. **Quantified entry**: Every engagement starts with measurable waste, not vague "digital transformation"
4. **Cross-vertical pattern reuse**: Compliance automation, data integration, and document intelligence patterns proven across 4 verticals
