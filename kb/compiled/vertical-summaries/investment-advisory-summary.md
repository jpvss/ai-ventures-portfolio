## Our Positioning

- **Target ICP**: Mid-market escritorios, R$1--15B AuC, 10--80 advisors, 1,000--10,000+ clients
- **Core problem**: 5--12 disconnected data sources; advisors spend 6--10 hours/week on manual reports
- **Entry strategy**: CVM 179 compliance pressure -- commission transparency mandate forces firms to demonstrate value beyond product distribution
- **Differentiator**: Platform-agnostic (works across XP, BTG, Safra) with pre-built patterns from multiple engagements

## Key Offerings

### Paid Diagnostic (R$25K--R$80K, 2--3 weeks)

Assesses data maturity, advisor productivity, compliance gaps (CVM 178/179), and client retention risk.

## Competitive Advantage

1. **CVM 179 urgency**: Commission transparency (effective November 2024) forces escritorios to justify their fees with data-driven value
2. **Platform-agnostic**: Unlike XP Hub tools, our solutions unify data across XP, BTG, Safra, and independent platforms
3. **End-to-end consulting**: Not just tools (Gorila, SmartBrain) -- integrated data pipelines, AI models, and compliance automation
4. **Open Finance leverage**: Pluggy/Phase 4 integration enables true client 360 across institutions
