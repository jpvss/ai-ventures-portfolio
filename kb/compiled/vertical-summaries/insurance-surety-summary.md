## Our Positioning

- **Target ICP**: Mid-market seguro garantia insurers and specialized corretoras
- **Core problem**: Legacy system integration -- 69% of insurers cite this as the #1 barrier to AI impact (CNseg/EY Feb 2026)
- **Entry strategy**: Rising sinistralidade (41.7%) makes AI-driven underwriting and claims intelligence urgent for profitability
- **Differentiator**: We build the data integration layer that makes AI work -- bridging policy admin, credit bureaus, SUSEP, and claims systems

1. **Integration expertise at scale**: 69% of insurers' #1 AI barrier is legacy integration -- our core competency
2. **Regulatory tailwinds**: Lei 14.133/2021 (30% step-in clause), Lei 15.040/2024 (response deadlines), OPIN Phase 3 all create demand
3. **Rising loss ratio urgency**: 41.7% sinistralidade (up 16pp) makes AI-driven claims and underwriting existential, not optional
4. **Digital-first competitive pressure**: Granto, Brick setting automation standards that traditional insurers must match
