## Our Positioning

- **Target ICP**: Auction platforms, leiloeiros, investor groups, professional investors, banks with property portfolios
- **Core problem**: Extreme data fragmentation -- 1,000+ heterogeneous sources, no centralized database, no unified property identifier
- **Entry strategy**: Quantified efficiency gains -- manual edital analysis at R$2,000/property vs. R$50/property with AI
- **Differentiator**: Full data infrastructure stack (aggregation + valuation + legal analysis + dashboards) vs. single-feature competitors
