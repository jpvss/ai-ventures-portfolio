# Auction Market — Sales Playbook

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|---------|
| Client Type | Auction platforms, aggregators, leiloeiros, professional investor groups/funds, advisory firms, law firms specializing in auctions |
| Scale | Platforms with 100+ active users or investor groups screening 50+ properties/month |
| Technology Maturity | Low to medium — still relying on spreadsheets, manual searches, and disconnected tools |
| Pain Indicators | Manual edital analysis, no centralized property database, limited valuation capabilities, high missed-opportunity rate |
| Decision Maker | Platform founder/CEO, fund manager, advisory firm partner |
| Budget Holder | Same as decision maker in most cases (smaller organizations); CTO/COO for larger platforms |
| Disqualifiers | Individual investors (too small), platforms with existing mature AI capabilities (rare) |

## Sales Hooks

### Hook 1: "You're missing 90% of opportunities."

**Context:** Use with investor groups and advisory firms who manually search a limited number of sites.
**Supporting Data:** With 1,000+ leiloeiro sites and no centralized database, even professional investors screen only a fraction of available deals. Our aggregation engine unifies 800+ sources.
**Follow-up Question:** "How many auction platforms do you currently monitor? And how confident are you that you're seeing the full market?"

### Hook 2: "R$2,000 per property analysis is killing your margins."

**Context:** Use with law firms and investor groups that rely on manual lawyer review for edital/matricula analysis.
**Supporting Data:** AI drops legal analysis cost from R$2,000 to ~R$50 per property, enabling screening of 200+ properties where they currently analyze 20.
**Follow-up Question:** "How many properties per month do you pass on because the legal analysis cost doesn't justify the potential return?"

### Hook 3: "The 15% sell-through rate reveals a broken market."

**Context:** Use with auction platforms and leiloeiros who want to improve their sell-through rates.
**Supporting Data:** Per ABRAIM data, only 15% of properties brought to auction actually sell. Better matching, valuation accuracy, and risk assessment dramatically improve outcomes for both sellers and buyers.
**Follow-up Question:** "What is your platform's current sell-through rate, and what do you think is preventing the other 85% from selling?"

### Hook 4: "Hidden costs eat your 40% discount."

**Context:** Use with investors who have been burned by unexpected costs (IPTU, condominio, penhoras) after arrematacao.
**Supporting Data:** Investors must reserve 40%+ above the lance value for hidden costs (ITBI 2-3%, commission 5%, cartorio 1-2%, legal 3-5%, hidden debts). Caixa spent R$443M in 2023 on IPTU and condominio alone for unsold properties. AI risk scoring identifies hidden debts before bidding.
**Follow-up Question:** "Have you ever discovered unexpected debts or encumbrances after winning an auction? What did that cost you?"

### Hook 5: "The market grew 86% but your tools didn't."

**Context:** Use with any prospect who acknowledges the market growth but hasn't invested in technology.
**Supporting Data:** 86% volume increase in 2024, 275,000 transactions, R$200B moved. Spreadsheets and manual searches cannot scale with volume that doubled in one year.
**Follow-up Question:** "How has your team's capacity kept pace with the market doubling? Are you screening more properties, or falling behind?"

### Hook 6: "From 4 hours to 30 seconds."

**Context:** Use to demonstrate the concrete, measurable impact of AI-powered valuation.
**Supporting Data:** Our AVM replaces manual comparable market analysis (cross-referencing FipeZap, OLX, ZAP, QuintoAndar by hand) with instant AI-powered valuation using data from 20+ real estate portals. Improves bid accuracy by 25%.
**Follow-up Question:** "How long does your team currently spend on property valuation? And how many properties could you evaluate if that dropped to 30 seconds each?"

## Discovery Call Framework

### Questions to Ask

1. How many properties do you screen per week/month? What is your conversion rate (screened to bid to won)?
2. Walk me through your process from discovering an auction listing to making a bid decision. How many hours and how many people are involved?
3. What is your current valuation methodology? How long does it take per property?
4. How do you assess legal risk? Do you use lawyers for every property or only shortlisted ones?
5. How many auction platforms/leiloeiro sites do you currently monitor?
6. What is your average time from arrematacao to completed matricula registration?
7. What happened the last time you discovered a hidden debt or encumbrance after winning an auction?
8. How has the 86% market growth in 2024 affected your operations? Are you keeping up?

### Red Flags (Disqualifiers)
- Individual investor with budget under R$50K (better served by SaaS subscriptions like Leilao Ninja)
- No willingness to invest in technology (wants free consultation)
- Purely speculative interest with no current auction activity
- Organization with fewer than 5 properties/month screening volume

### Green Flags (High-Fit Indicators)
- Screening 50+ properties/month but only bidding on a fraction due to capacity limits
- Spending R$2,000+ per property on legal analysis
- Manually cross-referencing multiple valuation sources
- Experienced the pain of hidden costs/debts after arrematacao
- Platform with growing user base but aging technology stack
- Expressed frustration with the fragmentation of the auction market
