## Our Differentiation

### vs. Data Providers (DataZAP, Hiperdados, Datallog)

- **Implementation, not just data**: We build the pipeline that connects data to client workflows, not just a dashboard to look at
- **Multi-source integration**: We combine ITBI + FipeZAP + IBGE + cartorio + zoning; data providers typically offer one source
- **AI-native**: Document intelligence (matricula parsing, edital analysis) and agentic workflows (land prospecting agent) that data providers cannot match
- **Customized to client operations**: We integrate with Sienge/UAU/Vista, not just provide a standalone tool

### vs. Consulting Firms (Brain, Big 4)

- **Speed**: 2--3 week diagnostic vs. 8--12 week discovery phase
- **Price**: R$25K--R$75K diagnostic vs. R$100K--R$300K+ scoping
- **Automated systems**: We deliver platforms that run continuously, not one-time studies
- **Quantified entry**: Every diagnostic leads with specific R$ figures for waste and opportunity

### vs. Software Vendors (Sienge, UAU, Vista)

- **Cross-system intelligence**: We connect CRM, ERP, external data, and accounting -- vendors optimize only their own product
- **AI layer**: Document intelligence, ML valuation, lead scoring that no real estate ERP/CRM offers natively
- **Data enrichment**: We bring external data (ITBI, IBGE, FipeZAP, zoning) into client systems
- **SPE specialization**: We solve the patrimonio de afetacao consolidation problem that ERPs handle poorly

### vs. In-House Teams

- **Cross-client patterns**: We bring pre-built patterns from multiple incorporadora/assessoria engagements
- **Speed to value**: Pre-built AVM, edital parser, SPE engine deliver in 8--14 weeks vs. 6--12 months in-house
- **Cost efficiency**: R$100K--R$300K project vs. R$400K+/year for a senior data team
- **External data expertise**: Municipality-specific ITBI access, FipeZAP API, SREI integration require specialized knowledge

## Win/Loss Patterns

### Common Win Factors

- Leading with a specific pain point quantified in R$ (e.g., "200 hours/month on SPE reconciliation")
- Demonstrating live data integration (pulling ITBI data for the client's target city during the meeting)
- Showing pre-built edital parsing or matricula analysis in action
- Offering a low-risk paid diagnostic as entry point
- Referencing portfolio projects (florida-flip, leila-do-leilao, incorporacoes-gestao) for credibility

### Common Loss Factors

- Client has existing DataZAP subscription and conflates data access with data intelligence
- Internal IT team wants to build in-house (especially at R$300M+ incorporadoras)
- Budget cycle misalignment -- incorporadora CapEx tied to project launches, not IT investment
- Sienge/UAU vendor lock-in with "we'll build that in the next version" promises

## Market Signals

- **SREI rollout (2025--2026)**: Digital cartorio creating new data access opportunities; first movers gain advantage
- **DataZAP expanding B2B**: OLX investing in B2B data products; we must differentiate on implementation/AI
- **Sienge adding analytics**: Softplan exploring BI features; we must stay ahead on cross-system integration and AI
- **Leilao volume surge**: 40%+ growth creating urgent demand for auction intelligence technology
- **MCMV scale**: 2M+ units in pipeline means mid-market incorporadoras are scaling rapidly and hitting data infrastructure limits
