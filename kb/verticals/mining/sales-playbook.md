## Sales Hooks

### Hook 1: "You're leaving R$500K--R$2M on the table with CFEM miscalculations."

**Context:** Use when talking to CFO or finance director of any miner with R$200M+ revenue. CFEM is a universal pain point because every miner pays it monthly.
**Supporting Data:** Manual spreadsheet calculations have 2--5% error rates. For a R$800M iron ore miner paying 3.5% CFEM (~R$28M/year), that's R$560K--R$1.4M in overpayments annually. Underpayments carry a 30% surcharge penalty from ANM.
**Follow-up Question:** "How is your CFEM calculated today? How confident are you in the accuracy? When was the last time you audited the calculation against actual NF-e data?"

### Hook 2: "One missed environmental condicionante can shut you down tomorrow."

**Context:** Use with COO or environmental/compliance directors. Especially powerful in Minas Gerais where SEMAD enforcement has intensified post-Brumadinho.
**Supporting Data:** Most mid-market miners track license conditions in Excel. Operational halt costs R$500K--R$5M per day. A single 30-day suspension means R$15--150M in impact. Fines under Lei 9.605/98 range from R$50 to R$50M.
**Follow-up Question:** "How do you currently track your LP/LI/LO condicionantes? Is there one person who holds all that knowledge? What happens if they're unavailable?"

### Hook 3: "Post-Brumadinho, dam safety compliance carries criminal liability."

**Context:** Use with CEO or legal counsel. The personal criminal liability angle resonates with executives more than any financial argument.
**Supporting Data:** Lei 14.066/2020 applies to ALL mining tailings dams, not just large ones. PAE is mandatory. Non-compliance means personal criminal liability for executives. Real-time data submission to ANM is required via SNISB.
**Follow-up Question:** "Are all your tailings dams fully compliant with Lei 14.066/2020? Do you have real-time instrumentation data flowing to ANM? Is your PAE current and tested?"

### Hook 4: "Your fleet management system has data worth millions that nobody reads."

**Context:** Use with COO or mine operations director. Works especially well when you can observe their FMS dashboards and see underutilized capabilities.
**Supporting Data:** Most miners use only 30--40% of FMS capabilities. Properly analyzing this data yields 5--15% productivity improvement. For a 30-truck operation at 75% utilization, a 5--10% improvement delivers R$10--25M/year in additional value.
**Follow-up Question:** "What percentage of your FMS data do you actively analyze? When was the last time dispatch optimization insights changed a production decision?"

### Hook 5: "Your geological data and production data don't talk to each other."

**Context:** Use with chief geologist or mine planning director. This resonates with technical leaders who understand the value of grade reconciliation.
**Supporting Data:** Grade dilution from poor blending costs 2--5% of revenue. Proper data integration between block models, drilling data, assay results, and production data enables ML-powered blending optimization.
**Follow-up Question:** "How do you currently reconcile planned grade with actual delivered grade? What's your mine-to-mill reconciliation process?"

## Discovery Call Framework

### Questions to Ask

1. "Walk me through your CFEM calculation process -- who does it, what tools, how many hours?"
2. "How many different systems does your operations team use daily? Do they share data automatically?"
3. "How do you track environmental license condicionantes and their deadlines?"
4. "What percentage of your fleet management system's capabilities are you actively using?"
5. "How does geological data flow from drilling to mine planning to production?"
6. "What's your biggest compliance concern right now -- ANM, IBAMA, or NR-22?"
7. "Have you explored BNDES financing options for digital transformation?"
8. "How long does your annual RAL preparation take?"

### Red Flags (Disqualifiers)

- Revenue below R$50M (insufficient scale for meaningful ROI)
- No ERP system in place (data foundation missing)
- Active litigation or regulatory shutdown (too much chaos for a consulting engagement)
- Internal IT team with explicit mandate to build all solutions in-house and executive backing
- No executive sponsorship -- purely IT-driven inquiry

### Green Flags (High-Fit Indicators)

- CFO or COO expressing frustration with CFEM accuracy or compliance burden
- Recent ANM audit or IBAMA inspection that exposed gaps
- BNDES financing approved or in process for digital investment
- Mining Hub membership or attendance at IBRAM events (innovation-minded)
- Multiple active environmental licenses with complex condicionantes
- Fleet of 20+ trucks with underutilized FMS
- Post-Brumadinho dam safety concerns expressed by executives
- Recent NR-22 compliance review that identified gaps
