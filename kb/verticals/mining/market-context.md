# Mining Consulting — Market Context

- **Brazil mineral sector revenue**: R$248.2B (2023)

- **Global digital mining market**: growing at 9.8% CAGR to reach $18.1B by 2030
- **BNDES mining fund**: R$1B targeting mining companies under R$300M revenue

## Industry Structure

Mid-market Brazilian miners (R$100M--R$2B revenue) represent the sweet spot for AI consulting. These companies are large enough to have complex operations across geology, mine planning, production, fleet management, environmental compliance, and finance -- but lack the resources of industry giants like Vale to build in-house digital teams.

The typical mid-market miner operates with **5--8 disconnected systems**:
- ERP: TOTVS Protheus (~60% penetration) or SAP Business One
- Mine planning: GEOVIA Surpac, Deswik, Vulcan, or Datamine
- Fleet management: Modular DISPATCH, Cat MineStar, Wenco, or Hexagon Jigsaw
- Environmental monitoring: standalone spreadsheets
- Lab data: LIMS systems
- Financial data: ERP-confined

The result: geological models never flow automatically to production systems, CFEM royalty calculations happen in Excel, and environmental license compliance is tracked manually.

## Technology Adoption Landscape

Real examples of AI adoption are emerging in Brazilian mining:

- **Nexa Resources**: Databricks for AI-driven operational insights
- **CBA**: AI-powered water treatment at Mirai plant -- 80% reduction in chemical usage
- **Vale**: Autonomous trucks at Brucutu -- 11% productivity gains, zero accidents
- **Mining Hub (Belo Horizonte)**: Connects 22+ mining companies with innovation providers

Despite these lighthouse examples, mid-market miners use only **30--40% of their fleet management system capabilities**, and most compliance reporting remains manual.

## Data Maturity Gap

Mid-market miners suffer from a profound data maturity gap:

| Dimension | Current State | Impact |
|-----------|--------------|--------|
| Data Integration | 5--8 disconnected systems | No automated flow between geology, planning, production, sales, finance |
| CFEM Calculation | Excel spreadsheets | 2--5% error rates, overpayments of R$560K--R$1.4M/year or 30% surcharge penalties |
| Environmental Compliance | Manual tracking | Risk of license suspension (R$500K--R$5M/day operational halt) |
| Fleet Management | 30--40% capability utilization | Missed 5--15% productivity improvement opportunity |
| Geological Data | Siloed from production | Grade dilution from poor blending costs 2--5% of revenue |
| Safety/NR-22 | Mostly manual records | Incident tracking, PGR, CIPAMIN managed in spreadsheets |

## Macro Trends

1. **Digital compliance becoming mandatory**: ANM's PGRM platform (launched March 2025 by Serpro) requires digital CFEM/DIEF submission
2. **Post-Brumadinho regulatory tightening**: Lei 14.066/2020 mandates PAE for ALL mining tailings dams, with personal criminal liability for executives
3. **CFEM reform (Lei 13.540/2017)**: Moved from receita liquida to receita bruta minus specific taxes, with substance-specific aliquots (1%--3.5%)
4. **ESG pressure**: Growing demand for GRI/SASB/ISSB reporting and ESG-linked financing
5. **BNDES funding**: R$1B fund specifically targeting miners under R$300M revenue creates budget pathway for digital transformation

## Opportunity Sizing

The addressable market for AI Ventures in mining combines:

- **Compliance automation**: Every mid-market miner needs CFEM automation (R$150K--R$350K per engagement) and environmental compliance dashboards (R$200K--R$500K)
- **Operational optimization**: Fleet analytics (R$300K--R$700K) and geological AI (R$400K--R$1M) for miners with open-pit operations
- **ESG/Safety**: Growing regulatory pressure creates demand for ESG platforms (R$250K--R$500K)
- **Diagnostic pipeline**: R$25K--R$75K paid diagnostics serve as the entry point

A single mid-market miner engagement can generate R$500K--R$2M+ in first-year revenue across diagnostic and 2--3 implementation projects.
