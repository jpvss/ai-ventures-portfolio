## Our Differentiation

### vs. Traditional Consulting Firms (Big 4, Accenture)

- **Speed**: 3--4 week diagnostic vs. 8--12 week discovery phase
- **Price**: R$25K--R$75K diagnostic vs. R$200K+ scoping engagement
- **AI-native**: We build automated systems, not PowerPoint recommendations
- **Mid-market fit**: Our engagement model (R$150K--R$1M projects) matches mid-market budgets
- **Quantified entry**: Every diagnostic leads with specific R$ figures for waste and opportunity

### vs. Software Vendors (TOTVS, Hexagon, Dassault)

- **Integration focus**: We connect the 5--8 systems that vendors leave siloed
- **Vendor-agnostic**: We work across TOTVS, Surpac, Deswik, DISPATCH, MineStar -- vendors only optimize their own product
- **Analytics layer**: We build the intelligence layer that sits on top of existing systems
- **Compliance expertise**: Software vendors don't understand CFEM calculation nuances or environmental condicionante tracking

### vs. In-House Teams

- **Cross-client intelligence**: We bring patterns from multiple mining engagements that in-house teams cannot access
- **Speed to value**: Pre-built patterns (CFEM engine, condicionante tracker) deliver in 8--12 weeks vs. 6--12 months for in-house
- **Cost efficiency**: R$150K--R$350K project vs. R$500K+/year for a senior data team
- **BNDES alignment**: Our engagements align with BNDES's R$1B fund for miners under R$300M revenue

## Win/Loss Patterns

### Common Win Factors

- Leading with a specific, quantified pain point (e.g., "R$1.2M in CFEM overpayments")
- Demonstrating understanding of ANM/IBAMA regulatory specifics
- Offering a low-risk paid diagnostic as entry point
- Showing pre-built patterns that reduce implementation risk
- Referencing real industry examples (Nexa/Databricks, CBA/AI water treatment, Vale/autonomous trucks)

### Common Loss Factors

- Client has strong internal IT team that wants to build in-house
- Existing vendor relationship locks out alternative approaches (e.g., TOTVS pushing their own analytics)
- Budget cycle misalignment -- mining CapEx cycles can delay decisions
- Lack of executive sponsorship when IT leads the conversation
