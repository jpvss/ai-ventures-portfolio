## Our Differentiation

### vs. SaaS Platforms (Exakt, Crawly)

- **AI-native**: We use LLMs for edital analysis, not just keyword matching. Our models extract requirements, deadlines, qualification criteria, and seguro garantia needs.
- **Cross-source integration**: We aggregate PNCP + state portals + Diarios Oficiais + sanction databases into one platform. SaaS tools typically cover one source.
- **Custom intelligence**: We build procurement intelligence tailored to client's specific CNAE codes, geographic markets, and competitive positioning -- not one-size-fits-all dashboards.
- **Audit capability**: SaaS platforms serve suppliers; we also serve TCEs and Controladorias with anomaly detection and audit intelligence.

### vs. Government In-House (TCU Alice/Adele)

- **Accessibility**: TCU invested R$50M+ and years building Alice/Adele. We deliver similar capabilities to state TCEs in 12--16 weeks at R$200K--R$500K.
- **Cross-entity intelligence**: In-house tools see only their own data. We bring patterns from multiple government entities and private sector engagements.
- **Speed to value**: Government IT procurement cycles take 6--18 months. Our diagnostic-first model delivers value in 2--3 weeks.

### vs. Traditional Consulting (Big 4)

- **Speed**: 2--3 week diagnostic vs. 8--12 week discovery phase
- **Price**: R$25K--R$75K diagnostic vs. R$200K+ scoping engagement
- **Deliverables**: Working software, not PowerPoint. Automated systems, not manual recommendations.
- **AI expertise**: Native LLM and ML capabilities vs. outsourced to technology partners

### vs. Municipal Systems (Betha/Fly/IPM)

- **Analytics layer**: We build intelligence on top of existing transaction systems, not replace them
- **Cross-entity**: Municipal systems serve one entity. We aggregate across entities for intelligence.
- **AI capabilities**: No municipal system vendor offers LLM-powered edital analysis or ML anomaly detection

## Win/Loss Patterns

### Common Win Factors

- Leading with a specific, quantified pain point (e.g., "you're screening 1% of contracts")
- Demonstrating knowledge of Lei 14.133/2021 transition challenges and PNCP capabilities
- Offering a low-risk paid diagnostic as entry point
- Showing licitaleads or ceap-deputy-expenses as proof of execution in GovTech
- Referencing TCU Alice/Adele as validation of AI for audit concept

### Common Loss Factors

- Government procurement cycles for consulting services (6--18 months)
- Requirement for licitacao to hire consultants (Lei 14.133/2021 applies to buying too)
- Strong internal IT team at larger entities (TCU, CGU) that want to build in-house
- Budget restrictions at smaller municipalities
- Political transitions disrupting project continuity

### Objection Patterns

| Objection | Response | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| "Government procurement of consulting is slow" | We can start with a diagnostic under dispensa de licitacao (below R$59K threshold under Lei 14.133 Art. 75). This builds the case for the larger implementation procurement. | Lei 14.133/2021 dispensa thresholds |
| "We don't have budget for AI" | TCU's Alice proved AI saves auditor time 10x. Our diagnostic (R$25K--R$75K) quantifies the specific ROI for your entity. ENAP and innovation funds may be available. | TCU Alice results, ENAP programs |
| "Our data isn't ready" | That's exactly what the diagnostic assesses. Most government entities already have rich data in PNCP, SIAFI, and local systems -- they just need an integration and analytics layer. | Typical data maturity findings |
| "We can use free tools (Querido Diario, Portal da Transparencia)" | Those are data sources, not intelligence. We build the analytics, anomaly detection, and actionable alerts on top of those same sources. | Data vs. intelligence distinction |

## Market Signals

- **PNCP API expansion**: As more entities publish on PNCP, data availability improves and analytics value increases
- **TCE modernization wave**: Multiple state TCEs are investing in digital transformation -- TCE-SP, TCE-MG, TCE-RS leading
- **BrazilLAB Demo Day pipeline**: GovTech startups graduating from BrazilLAB need data infrastructure partners
- **Election cycle effects**: New administrations (municipal 2024, state/federal 2026) drive transparency and accountability demand
- **Anti-corruption enforcement**: Continued enforcement of Lei 12.846/2013 drives corporate compliance spending
