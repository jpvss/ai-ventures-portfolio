# Insurance & Surety Bonds — Sales Playbook

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|---------|
| Company Type | Mid-market seguro garantia insurer or specialized corretora |
| Premium Volume | R$100M–R$1B+ in annual premiums (insurers) |
| Employee Count | 50–500 employees |
| Technology Maturity | Legacy core systems (Guidewire, Sinqia, or custom), limited API capabilities, manual underwriting and compliance workflows |
| Pain Indicators | Rising sinistralidade, slow policy issuance (days not minutes), manual SUSEP reporting, no OPIN readiness, losing deals to faster competitors |
| Decision Maker | Head of Underwriting, CTO/IT Director, COO |
| Budget Holder | CEO, CFO (with CTO/COO sponsorship) |

## Sales Hooks

### Hook 1: "Your underwriting takes days; the market demands minutes."

**Context:** Use when the prospect's competitors (especially digital-first players like Granto) are winning business through speed. Particularly effective for seguro garantia tied to licitações, where tomadores need bonds fast.

**Supporting Data:** Granto processes cotações in 24 hours. Embedded real-time issuance is becoming table stakes. Companies losing licitações because they can't get a garantia fast enough go to competitors. Our implementation reduces policy issuance from 5 days to 30 minutes for standard risks.

**Follow-up Question:** "What is your current average time-to-issue for a standard seguro garantia? How many cotação requests do you lose because the tomador went to a faster competitor?"

### Hook 2: "R$6.27B market growing 23% — but your systems can't scale."

**Context:** Use when the prospect acknowledges growth but struggles with operational capacity. Legacy systems built for lower volumes can't handle doubling demand.

**Supporting Data:** The seguro garantia market has doubled since 2020 and is projected to surpass R$10B by 2028. Every rejected application due to slow processing is lost revenue. Our underwriting engine increases throughput by 40% without additional headcount.

**Follow-up Question:** "What percentage of cotação requests do you decline or fail to respond to within the required timeframe? How has your operational capacity scaled relative to market growth?"

### Hook 3: "Sinistralidade rose 16 points to 41.7% — can you afford not to have AI claims intelligence?"

**Context:** Use when sinistralidade / loss ratio is top-of-mind for the prospect. This is the highest-ROI project in the vertical.

**Supporting Data:** A 5% loss ratio reduction on R$500M premium base = R$25M saved. The claims intelligence system pays for itself in Q1. Our implementation has achieved 5–8 percentage point loss ratio reduction and 67% improvement in claims resolution time (45 days to 15 days).

**Follow-up Question:** "What is your current sinistralidade? How has it trended over the past 3 years? What is your current fraud detection capability?"

### Hook 4: "80% of insurers use AI, but only 23% see real impact."

**Context:** Use when the prospect has tried AI before and is skeptical, or when they're considering buying an AI tool from a vendor. The CNseg/EY study validates that the problem isn't AI technology — it's integration.

**Supporting Data:** CNseg/EY February 2026 study: 80% of Brazilian insurers have adopted AI, but only 23% see meaningful impact. The main barrier is legacy system integration (69%). We specialize in exactly this — connecting AI to existing underwriting, claims, and compliance systems.

**Follow-up Question:** "Have you invested in AI tools before? What was the result? Where did the implementation stall?"

### Hook 5: "Open Insurance Phase 3 is live — compete or be disrupted."

**Context:** Use when the prospect hasn't started OPIN preparation, or when they're in S1/S2 segments with mandatory participation. Service initiation means customers can contract through any platform — first-movers gain the acquisition channel.

**Supporting Data:** OPIN Phase 3 (service initiation) went live in 2025, making Brazil a global pioneer in open insurance. Resolução CNSP 415/2021 mandates participation for S1/S2 segment insurers. FAPI-certified APIs, mTLS, OAuth 2.0, DCR compliance required.

**Follow-up Question:** "What is your current OPIN readiness? Do you have FAPI-certified APIs? Have you thought about OPIN as a customer acquisition channel or just a compliance burden?"

## Discovery Call Framework

### Questions to Ask

1. What is your current annual premium volume in seguro garantia? How has it grown over the past 3 years?
2. Walk me through your underwriting process from cotação request to policy issuance. How many manual steps? How long does it take?
3. What data sources do you use for credit analysis? How are they accessed — manual lookups or API?
4. What is your current sinistralidade? How does your claims process work today?
5. How do you currently report to SUSEP? How much time does your compliance team spend on reporting?
6. What is your OPIN readiness? Are you S1/S2 (mandatory participation)?
7. Have you invested in AI or automation before? What happened?
8. How do you manage broker relationships? Is data exchange manual or automated?
9. What core systems are you running? (Policy admin, CRM, claims, financial)
10. What is your biggest operational bottleneck right now?

### Red Flags (Disqualifiers)

- Premium volume under R$50M (may not justify investment)
- No executive sponsorship — only IT exploring without business buy-in
- Currently mid-migration to new core system (timing conflict)
- Unwilling to share operational data during diagnostic
- Exclusively focused on cost-cutting with no interest in revenue growth or risk management

### Green Flags (High-Fit Indicators)

- Rising sinistralidade or recent claims surprises
- Losing cotação requests to faster competitors
- Recent failed SUSEP audit or compliance concerns
- CEO/COO or Head of Underwriting is the contact (business-driven, not just IT)
- Growing premium volume but operational capacity is plateauing
- OPIN deadline approaching with no readiness plan
- Previously tried AI but struggled with integration
