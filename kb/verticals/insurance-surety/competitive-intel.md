## Our Differentiation

### vs. Traditional Consulting Firms (Accenture, Deloitte, EY)

- **Specialized depth**: We focus exclusively on AI-native data infrastructure for mid-market Brazilian insurers, not generic digital transformation
- **Speed to value**: 2–4 week paid diagnostic vs. 8–12 week unpaid discovery. First implementation delivers ROI in 3–6 months, not 12–18
- **Hands-on technical delivery**: We build the systems, not just the PowerPoints. Our team writes the ML models, API integrations, and dashboards
- **Brazilian regulatory expertise**: Deep knowledge of SUSEP circulars, OPIN requirements, Lei 15.040/2024 — not generic insurance knowledge

### vs. Software Vendors (Guidewire, Sinqia, Salesforce)

- **Integration-first, not replacement**: We connect and enhance existing systems rather than requiring expensive rip-and-replace of core platforms
- **AI-native**: Our solutions start with ML/AI as the core, not as an add-on module to legacy software
- **Flexible architecture**: We build on open standards and APIs, not proprietary lock-in
- **Domain-specific models**: Our underwriting and claims models are trained on Brazilian seguro garantia data, not generic global insurance data

### vs. In-House Teams

- **Specialized expertise**: AI/ML + insurance domain + Brazilian regulatory knowledge is an extremely rare combination in-house
- **Speed**: We deliver in months what in-house teams take years to build because we bring reusable patterns across clients
- **Cost efficiency**: A full in-house AI team (data engineers, ML engineers, domain experts) costs R$1.5M+/year; our engagement delivers more for less
- **Cross-client intelligence**: Patterns and learnings from multiple insurer engagements accelerate every project

### vs. Insurtechs (Granto, Brick, Segarante)

- **Complementary, not competitive**: We help traditional insurers compete with insurtechs, not replace insurtechs
- **Custom solutions**: Insurtechs offer standardized products; we build solutions tailored to each insurer's specific systems, processes, and strategy
- **Full-stack integration**: We connect the entire data landscape; insurtechs solve specific point problems

## Win/Loss Patterns

### Common Win Factors
- Prospect has acute pain: rising sinistralidade, failed SUSEP audit, losing deals to faster competitors
- Decision-maker is Head of Underwriting or CTO (technical buyer who understands integration challenges)
- Client has tried and failed with generic consulting or vendor-led implementation
- Regulatory deadline creates urgency (OPIN Phase 3, Lei 15.040/2024 compliance)

### Common Loss Factors
- Client wants to wait for vendor (Guidewire, Sinqia) to add AI features natively
- Budget holder is CFO focused only on cost, not on revenue/efficiency impact
- Client's core systems are so legacy that integration is prohibitively expensive (pre-qualify)
- Competing with in-house team that has strong executive sponsorship
