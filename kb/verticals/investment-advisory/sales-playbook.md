# Investment Advisory — Sales Playbook

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|---------|
| AuC Range | R$1B–R$15B |
| Advisor Count | 10–80 advisors |
| Client Count | 1,000–10,000+ |
| Platform | XP, BTG, Safra, or multi-platform |
| Technology Maturity | 5–12 disconnected data sources; CRM underutilized; reporting manual |
| Pain Indicators | Advisors spending 6–10 hrs/week on reports; churn >8%; CVM 179 compliance gaps; multi-platform data silos |
| Decision Maker | Managing partner, CEO, or COO |
| Budget Holder | Managing partner or finance director |
| Revenue Model | Transitioning from commission-based (0.5–0.7% ROA) to fee-based |

## Sales Hooks

### Hook 1: "Your advisors spend 30%+ of their time on non-revenue activities."

**Context:** Opening statement for any mid-market escritório — universally true. Best used when you can personalize with their advisor count.
**Supporting Data:** 20 advisors x 8 hrs/week x R$150/hr = R$24,000/week wasted on reports and data entry. That is R$1.25M/year in lost productive capacity.
**Follow-up Question:** "How many hours per week do your advisors spend compiling client reports?"

### Hook 2: "CVM 179 transparency is a threat or an opportunity."

**Context:** Use when discussing regulatory changes or when the prospect mentions compliance concerns. Especially powerful post-November 2024.
**Supporting Data:** Clients now see exactly what they pay in commissions. Escritórios that demonstrate superior value through AI-powered insights retain clients; those that cannot lose clients to robo-advisors charging 0.1–0.5%.
**Follow-up Question:** "How are you currently demonstrating value to clients who can now see every real they pay you in commissions?"

### Hook 3: "XP's own data shows CRM users capture 2.5x more clients."

**Context:** Use when the prospect's CRM adoption is low or when discussing growth. Reference XP's Ultra Advisors program.
**Supporting Data:** Ultra Advisors program participants increased captation by 53%. XP data shows 7x NPS improvement from proper CRM usage. Yet most offices have <50% CRM adoption among advisors.
**Follow-up Question:** "What percentage of your advisors consistently use CRM? And does your CRM tell them what to do next, or just store contact info?"

### Hook 4: "You're losing R$X million/year to preventable churn."

**Context:** Use when you can estimate or learn the prospect's churn rate. Most powerful when personalized with their numbers.
**Supporting Data:** Formula: AuC x churn rate x ROA = annual revenue at risk. Example: R$5B AuC x 12% churn x 0.6% ROA = R$3.6M/year in lost revenue. Reducing churn by 5 percentage points saves R$1.5M/year.
**Follow-up Question:** "Do you know your current churn rate? And when you lose a client, do you find out before or after they leave?"

### Hook 5: "The fee-based transition requires demonstrating tangible value."

**Context:** Use with offices actively moving to fee-based models (like Blue3) or discussing the industry's future.
**Supporting Data:** Blue3 has 20% of its base on fee-based, targeting 50%. When clients pay a visible fee instead of hidden commissions, they expect a visible service upgrade. AI-powered insights, proactive rebalancing alerts, and personalized reporting become the differentiator that justifies the fee.
**Follow-up Question:** "What percentage of your clients are on fee-based today? And what 'premium service' do you offer them that commission-based clients don't get?"

## Discovery Call Framework

### Questions to Ask

1. How many advisors do you have, and what is your total AuC?
2. How many hours per week does each advisor spend on manual reporting?
3. What platforms do you work with (XP, BTG, Safra, others)?
4. What is your current churn rate, and how do you detect at-risk clients?
5. How are you handling CVM 179 commission disclosure requirements?
6. What CRM do you use, and what percentage of advisors use it consistently?
7. How do you segment clients for communication and service levels?
8. What is your lead conversion rate, and how do you prioritize leads?
9. Do you have a compliance officer? How do they track suitability compliance (CVM 30)?
10. Are you considering or already transitioning to fee-based revenue?

### Red Flags (Disqualifiers)

- AuC below R$500M (insufficient budget for implementation projects)
- Single advisor (solo practice — no scale for automation ROI)
- No interest in technology / partners view tech as pure cost center
- Already heavily invested in a competing full-stack solution and satisfied
- No compliance officer or awareness of CVM 179 obligations

### Green Flags (High-Fit Indicators)

- Multi-platform operations (XP + BTG or others) creating data silos
- Churn rate above 10% and awareness of the problem
- Partners who have attended XP Ultra Advisors or similar programs (tech-aware)
- Active discussion about fee-based model transition
- Recent regulatory stress (CVM 179 compliance scramble)
- Growing advisor count (scaling pain makes automation urgent)
- Loss of a senior advisor who took clients (data ownership wake-up call)
