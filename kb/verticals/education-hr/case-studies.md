# Education & HR — Case Studies

## Overview

This file contains projected engagement outcomes for the Education & HR vertical, based on solution pattern capabilities, market benchmarks, and cross-vertical engagement experience. As engagements are completed, this file will be updated with actual anonymized results.

**Note**: The cv10x project provides internal proof-of-concept data for CV parsing accuracy and skills extraction. These metrics inform the projections below.

## Projected Case Study 1: Outplacement Firm — CV Intelligence Platform

### Client Profile
- **Type**: Mid-size outplacement/recolocação consultancy
- **Size**: 15 career coaches, 150 active candidates/month
- **Revenue**: R$8-12M annual
- **Current process**: Each coach manually reviews and rewrites 4-5 CVs/day (2-3 hours per CV including analysis, rewriting, and ATS optimization)

### Engagement
- **Diagnostic**: 2 weeks, R$30K — Data audit, process mapping, AI opportunity assessment
- **Phase 1 (CV Intelligence)**: 8 weeks, R$90K — CV parsing, skills extraction, quality scoring, optimization suggestions
- **Phase 2 (Optimization Engine)**: 6 weeks, R$55K — Automated rewriting, ATS keyword optimization, multi-format output
- **Retainer**: R$12K/mo — Model maintenance, RAIS data updates, continuous improvement

### Projected Outcomes

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| CVs processed per coach/day | 4-5 | 40-50 (AI-assisted review) | 10x throughput |
| Time per CV (full analysis + rewrite) | 2-3 hours | 15-20 minutes (AI draft + human refinement) | 90% time reduction |
| ATS pass-through rate (client CVs) | 35-45% | 70-80% | 2x improvement |
| Candidate placement rate | 40-50% within 3 months | 55-65% within 3 months | +15pp improvement |
| Revenue per coach | R$45K/mo | R$75K/mo (more candidates, higher success rate) | 67% increase |
| Client capacity (active candidates) | 150/month | 400/month (without additional hires) | 2.7x capacity |

### ROI Calculation
- **Investment**: R$175K (diagnostic + Phase 1 + Phase 2) + R$144K/year (retainer) = R$319K Year 1
- **Revenue uplift**: 15 coaches x R$30K/mo additional capacity = R$450K/mo potential; conservative 30% capture = R$135K/mo = R$1.62M/year
- **Payback period**: ~2.5 months
- **Year 1 ROI**: ~400%

### Key Success Factors
- Senior coach involvement in calibrating quality scoring (human-AI alignment)
- Gradual rollout: start with 3 coaches, expand after validation
- Client CV dataset (1,000+ historical CVs) provides excellent training data

## Projected Case Study 2: Mid-Market ATS Platform — Screening API

### Client Profile
- **Type**: Growing ATS platform competing below Gupy
- **Size**: 80 employees, 300 client companies, processing 50K applications/month
- **Revenue**: R$20-35M ARR
- **Current process**: Boolean keyword matching; clients complain about screening quality; losing 3-5 clients/quarter to Gupy

### Engagement
- **Diagnostic**: 3 weeks, R$50K — Full technical audit of matching algorithm, data quality assessment, competitive gap analysis
- **Phase 1 (Matching Engine)**: 10 weeks, R$150K — Semantic matching API, skills graph, bias audit pipeline
- **Phase 2 (Advanced Features)**: 6 weeks, R$70K — Configurable weights, admin dashboard, client SDK
- **Retainer**: R$22K/mo — Model retraining, bias monitoring, feature evolution

### Projected Outcomes

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Matching accuracy (qualified candidates surfaced) | 50-55% | 82-88% | +30pp improvement |
| False rejection rate (qualified candidates rejected) | 35-40% | 10-15% | 65% reduction |
| Client-reported screening satisfaction | 3.2/5 | 4.3/5 | +34% satisfaction |
| Client churn rate (quarterly) | 8-12% | 4-6% | 50% churn reduction |
| New client win rate (vs. Gupy) | 20% | 40% | 2x competitive win rate |
| Average time-to-shortlist | 5-7 days | 1-2 days | 70% reduction |

### ROI Calculation
- **Investment**: R$270K (diagnostic + Phase 1 + Phase 2) + R$264K/year (retainer) = R$534K Year 1
- **Retained revenue** (reduced churn): 300 clients x 4% churn reduction x R$6K avg annual contract = R$72K/year
- **New revenue** (improved win rate): 20 additional wins/year x R$6K avg = R$120K/year
- **Upsell revenue** (AI screening premium): 100 clients adopt AI tier x R$2K/mo premium = R$2.4M/year
- **Payback period**: ~3 months (from AI tier upsell alone)
- **Year 1 ROI**: ~380%

### Key Success Factors
- A/B testing framework to prove matching improvement with real client data
- Bias audit results as marketing differentiator vs. Gupy's black box
- Phased rollout to top 20 clients first, then platform-wide

## Projected Case Study 3: Coding Bootcamp — Employability Assessment

### Client Profile
- **Type**: Full-stack coding bootcamp (ISA model)
- **Size**: 100 employees, 800 students/year, 6-month program
- **Revenue**: R$25-40M annual
- **Current process**: Completion certificate = "job ready"; no data-driven employability measurement; claims 85% employment rate but cannot substantiate

### Engagement
- **Diagnostic**: 2 weeks, R$35K — Skills-to-market alignment audit, outcome tracking gap analysis
- **Phase 1 (Skills Mapping)**: 8 weeks, R$100K — Student skills inventory, CBO + job market alignment, gap analysis engine, readiness dashboard
- **Phase 2 (Recommendations + Tracking)**: 6 weeks, R$55K — Development recommendations, employment outcome tracking, employer portal
- **Retainer**: R$15K/mo — Job market data updates, model retraining, cohort analytics

### Projected Outcomes

| Metric | Before | After | Improvement |
|--------|--------|-------|-------------|
| Employment rate substantiation | Anecdotal (self-reported surveys) | Data-backed (employer-confirmed) | Credibility step-change |
| Time to first employment | 2.5 months avg (estimate) | 1.8 months avg (targeted preparation) | 28% reduction |
| Student skills-to-market alignment score | Not measured | 72% avg at graduation (target: 85%) | New metric, actionable |
| Curriculum iteration speed | Annual review | Quarterly data-driven updates | 4x faster curriculum adaptation |
| Student enrollment conversion | 12% of leads | 16% of leads (data-backed outcome claims) | +33% conversion |
| Employer partnership close rate | 15% of outreach | 25% of outreach (validated candidates) | +67% improvement |

### ROI Calculation
- **Investment**: R$190K (diagnostic + Phase 1 + Phase 2) + R$180K/year (retainer) = R$370K Year 1
- **Enrollment uplift**: 800 students x 33% conversion improvement x R$15K avg tuition = R$3.96M incremental potential; conservative 20% attribution = R$792K
- **ISA collection improvement**: Better-prepared students get jobs faster → ISA payments start sooner → R$200K-R$400K cash flow improvement
- **Payback period**: ~4 months
- **Year 1 ROI**: ~200%

### Key Success Factors
- Integration with bootcamp's existing LMS for automated skills extraction
- Partnership with 5+ hiring companies for outcome validation
- Student buy-in on readiness assessments (positioning as help, not judgment)

## Cross-Case Patterns

### Common Success Factors
1. **Paid diagnostic creates commitment** — clients who pay R$25K-R$60K for diagnostic are 60-70% likely to proceed to implementation
2. **Quick-win prototypes close deals** — parsing 100 CVs or matching 50 candidates during diagnostic creates urgency
3. **Public data integration surprises clients** — most have never seen RAIS/CBO data applied to their business; it builds immediate credibility
4. **Phased delivery reduces risk perception** — clients can validate Phase 1 results before committing to Phase 2
5. **cv10x components accelerate delivery** — reusing production-tested code reduces Phase 1 timelines by 30-40%

### Common Risks and Mitigations
1. **Data quality worse than expected** → Add 2-week data cleaning phase; adjust scope
2. **Stakeholder misalignment** → Include alignment workshop in diagnostic; get written sign-off on priorities
3. **Model accuracy below threshold** → Set realistic thresholds in contract; include iteration budget
4. **LGPD compliance concerns delay project** → Include LGPD assessment in diagnostic; provide compliance framework upfront
5. **Champion leaves mid-project** → Build relationships with 2-3 stakeholders; document business case for continuity
