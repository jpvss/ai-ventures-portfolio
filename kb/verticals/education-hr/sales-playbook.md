# Education & HR — Sales Playbook

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

### Primary ICP: Mid-Market HR/EdTech with AI Gap

| Attribute | Criteria | Weight |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Company size | 20-300 employees | High |
| Revenue | R$5M-R$50M annual | High |
| Data volume | 100+ CVs/month or 500+ candidates/month or 200+ students/year | High |
| Current AI usage | None to basic (keyword matching, simple chatbots) | High |
| Growth rate | 15%+ YoY revenue or user growth | Medium |
| Tech stack | Cloud-hosted, API-capable, modern database | Medium |
| Decision-maker access | CTO, VP Product, or CEO directly reachable | High |
| Budget authority | Can approve R$50K-R$250K without board approval | Medium |
| Competitive pressure | Losing deals/clients to AI-powered competitors (esp. Gupy) | High |

### Segment-Specific ICP Criteria

| Segment | Additional Must-Have | Disqualifier |
|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| Outplacement firm | 50+ active candidates/month; coaches are capacity-bottlenecked | Fewer than 30 candidates/month (too small for ROI) |
| ATS platform | 100+ client companies; keyword matching is current approach | Already has semantic AI matching; no budget for R&D |
| Bootcamp/EdTech | 200+ students/year; claims employment outcomes but cannot prove | Fully accredited university (different buying process, long cycles) |
| Employment agency | 500+ candidates/month; manual matching process | Government-only contracts with no budget flexibility |

## Sales Hooks

### Hook 1: "The Gupy Gap" (for ATS platforms)
> "Gupy raised R$500M+ and has 800 engineers building AI screening. Your clients compare you to them every quarter. We give you Gupy-level AI matching in 10 weeks, not 10 years — as an API you plug into your existing platform."

**When to use**: ATS platform prospects who mention losing clients to Gupy or being asked about AI capabilities
**Follow-up question**: "How many clients have asked about AI screening in the last 6 months? What did you tell them?"

### Hook 2: "The Coach Bottleneck" (for outplacement firms)
> "Your best coach reviews 5 CVs per day at R$150/hour. Our AI reviews 500 and flags the 20 that need human attention — your coaches focus on the 20% that matters while 80% gets handled automatically."

**When to use**: Outplacement firms that are turning away clients or have waitlists
**Follow-up question**: "How many candidates are you turning away or putting on waitlists because your coaches are at capacity?"

### Hook 3: "The Employment Outcome Proof" (for bootcamps)
> "Every bootcamp claims 85-90% employment rates. None can prove it with data. We connect your curriculum to actual job market requirements from 500K+ live postings and track outcomes with employer confirmation. You go from 'we think graduates get jobs' to 'we can prove it.'"

**When to use**: Bootcamps in competitive markets (São Paulo, remote), especially those using ISA models where employment directly affects revenue
**Follow-up question**: "How do you currently measure employment outcomes? What percentage of graduates can you actually track?"

### Hook 4: "The Keyword Rejection Problem" (for ATS platforms and HR departments)
> "Your ATS rejected a candidate who wrote 'gestão de equipes' because the JD said 'liderança.' They are the same skill. Keyword matching misses 40% of qualified candidates. Our semantic matching understands meaning, not just strings."

**When to use**: Any prospect with keyword-based screening; especially effective as live demonstration
**Follow-up question**: "Can I show you what happens when we run your top 5 JDs against your last 100 rejected candidates?"

### Hook 5: "The RAIS Intelligence Advantage" (for any segment)
> "The government publishes detailed employment data — salaries, hiring volumes, skills demand — by occupation and region every month. Your competitors are not using it. We turn RAIS/CAGED into your competitive intelligence layer."

**When to use**: Prospects who lack market data capabilities; works across all segments
**Follow-up question**: "Do you currently have any systematic way to benchmark salaries or track labor market trends for your key occupations?"

### Hook 6: "The LGPD Compliance Advantage" (for ATS platforms)
> "When PL 2338 passes — and it will — every AI screening system will need explainability and bias audits. We build that in from day one. Your compliance becomes your marketing differentiator: 'We can explain every AI decision we make.'"

**When to use**: Prospects with compliance-conscious leadership; CTO or legal-influenced buying processes
**Follow-up question**: "Has your legal team reviewed your current screening algorithm for LGPD Art. 20 compliance? Can you explain to a candidate why they were rejected?"

## Outreach Strategy

### Channel Mix

| Channel | Approach | Expected Response Rate |
|---------|----------|----------------------|
| LinkedIn (direct) | Personalized message referencing specific pain point from their product/job posts | 8-12% response rate |
| Warm intro (portfolio network) | Introduction from existing client or industry contact | 25-35% meeting rate |
| Content marketing | "State of AI in Brazilian HR" report; cv10x case study; webinar on LGPD + AI hiring | Inbound leads, 2-5/month at scale |
| Industry events | ABRHBrasil, HR4results, Bett Educar, Web Summit Rio | 3-5 qualified leads per event |
| Partnerships | White-label AI capabilities for HR consultancies who refer clients | Ongoing lead flow |

### Outreach Sequence (Cold)

**Day 1**: LinkedIn connection request + personalized note (reference their product, recent hire, or public pain point)
**Day 3**: If connected, send value message with relevant insight (not a pitch) — e.g., "I noticed [their ATS] still uses keyword matching — here's what we found when testing semantic vs. keyword on 10K Brazilian CVs"
**Day 7**: Share relevant content piece (case study, industry report, or cv10x demo)
**Day 14**: Direct ask for 25-minute discovery call with specific value proposition
**Day 21**: Final follow-up with time-limited offer (e.g., "We're doing free 30-minute AI readiness assessments for HR tech companies this month")

### Outreach Sequence (Warm Intro)

**Day 0**: Introduction email/message from mutual contact
**Day 1**: Follow-up with personalized context + specific value proposition
**Day 3**: Send relevant case study or demo
**Day 5**: Schedule discovery call

## Discovery Call Framework (25 minutes)

### Structure

**Minutes 1-3: Rapport + Context**
- "Thanks for the time. I saw [specific thing about their company]. Quick context on us: JP Ventures builds AI data infrastructure for mid-market companies. We have an active project (cv10x) in the CV/career space."

**Minutes 3-10: Pain Discovery (SPIN)**
- **Situation**: "Walk me through your current [recruitment/screening/assessment] process end-to-end"
- **Problem**: "Where do things break down? What takes longer than it should?"
- **Implication**: "What does that cost you? In lost clients? In missed candidates? In coach time?"
- **Need-payoff**: "If you could [solve specific problem], what would that mean for your business?"

**Minutes 10-18: Solution Alignment**
- Map their pain to one of our 5 solution projects
- Show relevant quick demo or example (CV parsed in 30 seconds, matching comparison, etc.)
- Reference cv10x capabilities and public data integration
- Mention LGPD compliance advantage if relevant

**Minutes 18-23: Qualification**
- "Who else would need to be involved in a decision like this?"
- "What's your typical timeline for technology investments?"
- "Have you evaluated other solutions? What happened?"
- "What budget range are you working within for this type of initiative?"

**Minutes 23-25: Next Steps**
- Propose paid diagnostic with specific scope and timeline
- "Our next step would be a 2-3 week diagnostic for R$[X]K. We map your data, processes, and AI opportunities, and deliver a roadmap with ROI projections. The diagnostic stands alone — it's valuable even if you don't proceed further."

## Objection Handling

### "We can build this ourselves"
> **Response**: "You absolutely could — and some companies do. The question is timeline and opportunity cost. Our CV Intelligence Platform takes 8 weeks with production-tested components from cv10x. Building from scratch? 6-12 months minimum, plus you need to hire 2-3 ML engineers at R$25K-R$45K/month each. That is R$600K-R$1.3M in the first year, with uncertain timelines. We deliver for R$60K-R$150K in 8-12 weeks."
> **Evidence**: Cost comparison spreadsheet; timeline comparison; hiring market data for ML engineers in Brazil

### "AI is not reliable enough for hiring decisions"
> **Response**: "You are right to be cautious — and we agree. That is why every system we build has human-in-the-loop by design. The AI handles the 80% that is mechanical (parsing, matching, scoring) and surfaces the 20% that needs human judgment. Your recruiters spend their time on high-value decisions, not data entry. Plus, we build in bias audits and explainability from day one — something most AI tools cannot offer."
> **Evidence**: Bias audit methodology; LGPD Art. 20 compliance framework; before/after accuracy metrics

### "We tried AI before and it did not work"
> **Response**: "What did you try? [Listen.] That is a common experience — early AI tools (2019-2022 era) were genuinely limited, especially for Portuguese. The technology has fundamentally changed in the last 18 months. GPT-4 and Claude handle Portuguese CV analysis at near-human quality now. We can demonstrate with your actual data in the diagnostic — if the quality is not there, you will know in 2 weeks, not 6 months."
> **Evidence**: Live demo with prospect's actual CVs/JDs; before/after comparison with their previous tool

### "Gupy already does this"
> **Response**: "Gupy is excellent — for companies that want to adopt their entire platform. But your clients chose your ATS for a reason: flexibility, pricing, your specific features. What they are asking for is Gupy-level AI intelligence without switching platforms. That is exactly what we build — AI capabilities that plug into YOUR platform via API, maintaining your brand, your UX, your client relationships."
> **Evidence**: API-first architecture diagram; customization vs. platform lock-in comparison

### "We do not have the budget right now"
> **Response**: "I understand. Two options: First, our diagnostic is R$25K-R$60K and delivers standalone value — it shows you exactly where AI can help, with ROI numbers, even if you implement later. Second, for the right opportunity, we can structure Phase 1 with a performance component — partial payment tied to hitting accuracy thresholds. We are confident enough in our delivery to share the risk."
> **Evidence**: ROI model showing 2-4 month payback; performance-based pricing structure

### "What about LGPD? We are worried about processing candidate data with AI"
> **Response**: "This is the right question and most companies are not asking it — which is a risk. We build LGPD compliance into every solution: consent management, data minimization, right to explanation (Art. 20), bias auditing, data retention policies. When PL 2338 passes requiring AI audit trails in hiring, your system will already be compliant. This is a competitive advantage, not a burden."
> **Evidence**: LGPD compliance checklist (from regulatory-map.md); DPIA template; Art. 20 explainability demo

## Pricing Strategy

### Pricing Principles
1. **Always paid diagnostic** — never free discovery or proof-of-concept. The diagnostic itself delivers value.
2. **Phase-gated implementation** — clients commit to Phase 1, decide on Phase 2 based on results
3. **Retainer for ongoing value** — model maintenance, data updates, continuous improvement
4. **Value-based pricing** — price anchored to client ROI, not our cost

### Pricing Summary

|----------|-----------|---------|---------|----------|----------------|
| CV Intelligence Platform | R$30K | R$90K | R$55K | R$12K/mo | R$319K |
| ATS Screening API | R$50K | R$150K | R$70K | R$22K/mo | R$534K |
| Employability Assessment | R$35K | R$100K | R$55K | R$15K/mo | R$370K |
| Talent Marketplace Intel | R$28K | R$120K | - | R$12K/mo | R$292K |
| Skills Gap Analytics | R$30K | R$95K | - | R$10K/mo | R$245K |

### Discount Policy
- **No discounts on diagnostic** — it is already priced for value, not cost
- **5-10% discount on Phase 1 if committed during diagnostic** — reward fast decision-making
- **Annual retainer commitment**: 10% discount vs. monthly
- **Multi-solution bundle**: 15% discount on second solution when purchased together
- **Never compete on price** — if a prospect is purely price-shopping, they are not our ICP

## Pipeline Management

### Stage Definitions

| Stage | Definition | Exit Criteria | Typical Duration |
|-------|-----------|---------------|-----------------|
| Lead | Identified prospect matching ICP | Contact information verified; initial research complete | - |
| Outreach | Active outreach in progress | Response received (positive or negative) | 1-3 weeks |
| Discovery | Discovery call scheduled or completed | Pain confirmed; solution mapped; budget indication | 1-2 weeks |
| Proposal | Diagnostic proposal sent | Proposal reviewed by decision-maker | 1-2 weeks |
| Diagnostic | Diagnostic in progress | Diagnostic deliverables accepted | 2-3 weeks |
| Implementation | Implementation proposal sent/accepted | Contract signed for Phase 1 | 1-2 weeks |
| Active | Implementation in progress | Phase completion | 8-20 weeks |
| Retainer | Ongoing retainer relationship | Monthly review | Ongoing |

### Target Pipeline Metrics (Year 1)
- **Leads generated**: 40-60 (from all channels)
- **Discovery calls**: 20-30
- **Diagnostics sold**: 8-12
- **Implementations closed**: 5-8
- **Retainers active**: 3-5
- **Average deal size**: R$150K-R$350K (diagnostic + Phase 1 + 6 months retainer)
- **Sales cycle**: 4-8 weeks (lead to diagnostic close); 2-4 weeks (diagnostic to implementation)
