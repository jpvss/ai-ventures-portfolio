# Construction — Sales Playbook

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Criteria |
|-----------|---------|
| Company Type | Mid-market construtora, construtora de obras publicas, or administradora de condominios |
| Revenue | R$10M-R$200M (construtoras); R$5M-R$100M (administradoras) |
| Employee Count | 50-500 employees |
| Project Volume | 5+ simultaneous active projects (construtoras); 30+ condominios (administradoras) |
| Technology Maturity | Sienge or TOTVS ERP (or spreadsheet-based), limited BIM, manual cost tracking, no licitacao monitoring tool |
| Pain Indicators | Budget overruns >10%, manual SINAPI lookups, monitoring <10 municipalities, NR-18 fines, missed permit renewals, back-office growing linearly with portfolio |
| Decision Maker | Director of Engineering, Commercial Director, Financial Director, Owner |
| Budget Holder | Owner, CFO (with technical director sponsorship) |

## Sales Hooks

### Hook 1: "You discover budget overruns 2-3 months late. By then, the damage is done."

**Context:** Use when the prospect manages cost control in spreadsheets. Mid-market construtoras typically reconcile budget vs. actual monthly at best, with some projects going 60+ days without a proper cost comparison.

**Supporting Data:** Industry average budget overrun for mid-market public works: 15-25%. Our Cost Control Dashboard detects variance in <24 hours, reducing overruns to 5-10%. On a R$50M annual project portfolio, that is R$2.5-7.5M in avoided overruns.

**Follow-up Question:** "When was the last time you discovered a budget overrun? How long after the spending actually happened did you find out? What did it cost you?"

### Hook 2: "There are thousands of editais published daily across 5,570 municipalities. How many are you seeing?"

**Context:** Use when the prospect relies on manual Diario Oficial searches or word-of-mouth for licitacao pipeline. Most mid-market firms cover 5-10% of addressable opportunities.

**Supporting Data:** PNCP now publishes all public procurement electronically. Our Intelligence Radar monitors 100% of publications, applies AI to extract construction-relevant editais, and matches them to your qualifications. Clients typically see 5-10x more qualified opportunities within the first month.

**Follow-up Question:** "How many municipalities do you currently monitor for licitacoes? How do you monitor them — manual search, subscription service, or personal networks? How many bids did you submit last year vs. how many relevant editais were published in your region?"

### Hook 3: "Decreto 10.306 is not optional. If you can't deliver BIM, you can't bid on federal contracts."

**Context:** Use when the prospect acknowledges BIM requirements but has not integrated BIM into their cost/scheduling workflows. Particularly effective for firms that want to compete for PAC infrastructure projects.

**Supporting Data:** Decreto 10.306/2020 Phase 2 (construction execution) is now mandatory for federal projects above R$10M. Phase 3 (full lifecycle BIM) comes in 2028. Mid-market firms that cannot integrate BIM with their cost and scheduling systems will be excluded from the fastest-growing segment of public construction.

**Follow-up Question:** "Have you had to use BIM on any recent projects? How do you currently get quantities from BIM models into your cost spreadsheets? How long does a quantity takeoff take for a typical project?"

### Hook 4: "Your cost engineers spend 60% of their time on manual SINAPI lookups. That is R$200-500K/year in wasted expertise."

**Context:** Use when the prospect prepares budgets for public works. SINAPI has 100K+ compositions that must be referenced manually for every budget item.

**Supporting Data:** Automated SINAPI matching reduces budget preparation time by 90%. Our system ingests monthly SINAPI updates automatically and maps budget items to the correct compositions, flagging any items priced >5% above reference. Cost engineers redirect their time from data lookup to analysis and decision-making.

**Follow-up Question:** "How many budgets does your cost engineering team prepare per month? How long does a typical budget take? What percentage of that time is spent on SINAPI lookups vs. actual cost analysis?"

### Hook 5: "NR-18 fines are R$2K-R$200K per infraction. How many infractions did you have last year?"

**Context:** Use when the prospect has had safety incidents, fines, or project shutdowns due to compliance failures. Effective for construtoras with multiple simultaneous construction sites where centralized compliance monitoring is impossible manually.

**Supporting Data:** Our Compliance Tracker digitizes NR-18 inspections, monitors environmental permits with 90/60/30-day renewal alerts, and uses computer vision for PPE detection. Clients reduce NR-18 fines by 90%+ and eliminate project shutdowns from missed permit renewals.

**Follow-up Question:** "How much did you pay in NR-18 fines last year? Have you ever had a project shut down due to a permit or license issue? How do you currently track compliance across your active sites?"

## Discovery Call Framework

### Questions to Ask

1. What is your annual revenue? How many simultaneous projects do you typically manage?
2. Walk me through how you prepare a budget for a public works licitacao. How long does it take? How do you reference SINAPI?
3. How do you monitor licitacao opportunities? How many municipalities do you cover? How do you hear about new editais?
4. How do you track budget vs. actual costs during project execution? How often is this comparison made?
5. When was the last time you discovered a significant budget overrun? How far after the spending had occurred?
6. What ERP or project management systems do you use? How integrated are they?
7. Have you used BIM on any projects? How do you get data from BIM models into your cost systems?
8. How do you manage NR-18 compliance across your sites? Have you had any fines or shutdowns recently?
9. How do you track environmental permits and building licenses across projects?
10. What is your biggest operational bottleneck right now? If you could fix one thing, what would it be?

### Red Flags (Disqualifiers)

- Revenue under R$10M (unlikely to justify investment)
- Single-project company (limited portfolio complexity)
- Owner not involved in technology decisions (no executive sponsorship)
- Currently mid-migration to new ERP (timing conflict — revisit in 6-12 months)
- No public works exposure (removes SINAPI/licitacao value propositions)
- Unwilling to share project cost data during diagnostic

### Green Flags (High-Fit Indicators)

- Recent budget overrun that caused financial pain
- Growing bid pipeline but struggling to prepare budgets fast enough
- Multiple NR-18 fines or a recent project shutdown from compliance failure
- Wants to compete for PAC/DNIT infrastructure contracts but lacks BIM capabilities
- Using Sienge but frustrated with limited analytics/intelligence
- Administradora growing portfolio but back-office costs growing proportionally
- Already knows about SINAPI gaps in their budgets but cannot address them systematically
- incorporacoes-gestao referral (warm introduction from existing client)
