# Energy & Infrastructure — Market Context

## Market Size and Growth

- **GD installed capacity**: 25+ GW (2025), making Brazil a global top-5 distributed solar market
- **Number of GD plants**: 2M+ across residential, commercial, and industrial segments
- **Consumer units (UCs)**: 2.5M+ receiving GD compensation credits
- **Cumulative investment**: R$100B+ since REN 482/2012 launched the GD framework
- **Annual growth rate**: 30--40% YoY in new connections
- **Mercado Livre de Energia**: R$200B+ annual market, opening progressively to smaller consumers
- **Solar module cost decline**: 80%+ reduction over the past decade, driving economic viability

## Industry Structure

The Brazilian energy mid-market has four key player types, all underserved by current technology:

### Integradores Solares (Solar Integrators)
- **Revenue**: R$5M--R$100M
- **Role**: Design, sell, and install solar systems for commercial/industrial/rural clients
- **Pain**: Manual lead generation, no CRM pipeline visibility, competitor quoting faster
- **Typical size**: 10--100 employees, 50--500 installations/year

### Gestoras de Usinas GD (GD Plant Managers)
- **Portfolio**: 5--50 plants (1--5 MW each)
- **Role**: Own/operate GD plants, manage UC portfolios, handle billing with distribuidoras
- **Pain**: Billing reconciliation across 100--500 UCs in Excel, no consolidated performance dashboard
- **Typical size**: 5--30 employees, R$10M--R$50M revenue

### Comercializadoras de Energia (Energy Traders)
- **Role**: Buy/sell energy in the Mercado Livre, manage contracts (CCEAR/CUSD)
- **Pain**: Contract management complexity, demand forecasting, settlement reconciliation with CCEE
- **Typical size**: 10--50 employees, R$50M--R$500M traded volume

### Energy Investment Funds
- **Role**: Finance GD plants, require portfolio performance reporting
- **Pain**: No standardized reporting, generation variance unexplained, manual due diligence
- **Typical size**: R$100M--R$1B AUM in GD assets

## Technology Adoption Landscape

Current technology usage in the Brazilian GD mid-market:

- **Monitoring**: SolarZ (market leader, inverter-agnostic), Growatt Cloud (Growatt inverters), Huawei FusionSolar (Huawei inverters), manufacturer-specific apps
- **Billing**: Excel spreadsheets (80%+ of gestoras), Eleva Energia (emerging), custom internal systems
- **CRM/Sales**: Genial Solar (integradores), generic CRMs (Pipedrive, HubSpot), Excel
- **Financial**: Omie, ContaAzul, TOTVS for larger players
- **Prospecting**: Manual Google searches, referral networks, no data-driven lead gen

Despite 30--40% annual market growth, most mid-market players operate with disconnected tools and manual workflows. The gap between operational complexity and tooling maturity is widening.

## Macro Trends

1. **Marco Legal da GD transition**: Fio B tariff (TUSD Fio B) phase-in for post-2023 plants creates billing complexity that cannot be managed manually at scale
2. **Mercado Livre expansion**: Threshold dropping from 500 kW progressively toward all consumers by 2028 -- massive opportunity for comercializadoras
3. **Storage/batteries**: BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems) entering Brazilian market; ANEEL studying regulatory framework for GD + storage
4. **GD remota**: Remote distributed generation (consumers offset energy from distant plants) growing rapidly, adding billing complexity
5. **Smart grid**: AMI (Advanced Metering Infrastructure) deployment by distribuidoras enabling real-time consumption data
6. **Green hydrogen**: Long-term trend; early projects in Ceara and Bahia attracting federal incentives
